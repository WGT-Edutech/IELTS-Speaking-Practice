How to Ace This

General

Only four things affect your score: Fluency, Vocabulary, Grammar, Pronunciation. Not body language. Not eye contact. Not smiling.

Part 1 Tip

Give 2-3 sentence answers. Never just say yes or no. If you do not understand a word, ask: What do you mean by...?

Part 2 Tip

The bullet points are hints, not requirements. Focus on the main topic. If stuck, add: how you felt, what others thought, or compare to the past.

Tech Topic

When describing technology, do not just list features. Explain how it changed your daily life. Personal impact impresses examiners more than technical specs.

⏱ 2:00
Describe a useful electronic device you would like to own.
You should say:
  • what it is
  • how it would help your life
  • if it would be expensive to buy
  • whether it would be difficult to learn
  • and explain why you would like it
